# Crop Advisory Sheet — Kharif & Rabi 2026 (TEST MATERIAL)

> Synthetic advisory sheet written for the sarvam-bench offline-agriculture test. Figures are plausible and modelled
> on typical Indian state-university package-of-practice recommendations, but this sheet is NOT an official
> recommendation. Do not use it for real farming decisions. The scheme in section 5 is FICTIONAL.

All quantities are per acre unless stated. 1 acre = 0.4 hectare.

## 1. Paddy (Kharif)
- Nursery sowing: 25 May to 15 June. Transplanting: 25 June to 15 July, using seedlings 21 to 25 days old.
- Seed rate: transplanted paddy 8 kg per acre; direct-seeded rice (DSR) 10 kg per acre.
- Spacing: 20 cm x 15 cm, 2 to 3 seedlings per hill.
- Fertiliser: DAP 50 kg, MOP 30 kg and zinc sulphate 10 kg as basal dose at transplanting. Urea 90 kg in total,
  given in three equal splits: at transplanting, at tillering (25 days after transplanting) and at panicle
  initiation (50 days after transplanting).
- Water: keep 5 cm of standing water; drain the field 10 days before harvest.
- Stem borer: act when dead hearts reach 5% of tillers or 1 egg mass per square metre is seen. Spray
  chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC at 60 ml per acre.
- Brown planthopper: act when there are 10 hoppers per hill. Drain the field for 3 days and spray pymetrozine 50 WG
  at 120 g per acre at the base of the plants.

## 2. Cotton (Kharif)
- Sowing: 1 June to 10 July, only after 75 to 100 mm of cumulative rainfall.
- Seed: Bt hybrid, 2 packets (450 g each) per acre, plus the non-Bt refuge seed supplied with the packet sown around
  the field border.
- Spacing: 90 cm between rows x 60 cm between plants.
- Fertiliser: SSP 150 kg and MOP 40 kg as basal dose. Urea 100 kg in total, in three equal splits: at sowing, 30 days
  and 60 days after sowing. If leaves turn reddish, spray magnesium sulphate 1 kg in 100 litres of water.
- Pink bollworm: install 4 pheromone traps per acre from 45 days after sowing. Act when catches reach 8 moths per trap
  per night for 3 nights in a row, or when 10% of flowers are rosetted. Spray profenofos 50 EC at 400 ml per acre.
- Whitefly: act at 8 to 10 adults per leaf. Spray diafenthiuron 50 WP at 240 g per acre.

## 3. Soybean (Kharif)
- Sowing: 20 June to 10 July, after at least 100 mm of rainfall.
- Seed rate: 30 kg per acre. Row spacing 45 cm; sow 3 cm deep.
- Seed treatment: thiram + carbendazim (2:1) at 3 g per kg of seed, then Rhizobium culture at 5 g per kg of seed.
- Fertiliser: DAP 50 kg, MOP 20 kg and bentonite sulphur 8 kg as basal dose. No urea top-dressing.
- Weeds: keep the crop weed-free for the first 45 days after sowing.
- Girdle beetle: act when 3 to 5 girdled plants are seen per metre of row. Spray thiacloprid 21.7 SC at 300 ml per acre.

## 4. Wheat (Rabi)
- Sowing: timely sowing 1 to 20 November; late sowing up to 15 December.
- Seed rate: timely sowing 40 kg per acre; late sowing 50 kg per acre.
- Fertiliser: DAP 55 kg and MOP 20 kg as basal dose. Urea 110 kg in total: half at sowing, half at the first irrigation.
- Irrigation: first irrigation at crown root initiation, 21 days after sowing; 5 to 6 irrigations in all.
- Aphid: act at 10 aphids per tiller. Spray imidacloprid 17.8 SL at 40 ml per acre.
- Yellow rust: at the first yellow stripes on leaves, spray propiconazole 25 EC at 200 ml per acre.

## 5. Kisan Beej Sahayata Yojana 2026 (FICTIONAL scheme, for testing only)
- Benefit: 50% subsidy on certified seed of paddy, cotton, soybean or wheat, up to Rs 2,000 per acre, for at most
  5 acres per farmer.
- How to apply: at the gram panchayat office or on the scheme app, by 31 July 2026 for kharif crops and by
  30 November 2026 for wheat.
- Documents: land record extract, bank passbook, and an identity document.

## 6. Spraying rules (all crops)
- Use 200 litres of water per acre for every spray.
- Do not spray if rain is expected within 6 hours. Spray in the early morning or evening.
- Wear gloves and a mask while spraying.
